03 / Meet your new teammate

More than an answer.
A request your team can work with.

From the first question to the details needed for follow-up, Elma helps move the conversation forward.

  1. 01

    Answer

    Answer resident questions using the documents and building information you provide.

  2. 02

    Receive

    Let residents explain what they need in text or voice, in the same conversation.

  3. 03

    Process

    Ask for missing details and fill a structured request. The resident reviews and submits it.

  4. 04

    Help coordinate

    Give staff the submitted details and conversation to review, update the request status and plan follow-up.

Your team stays responsible for decisions, assignments and the work itself.
